About

Our story.

BetterBuilt was founded by Spencer Foreman, who has spent almost four years managing the online store for a national children's book publisher: product launches, campaigns, performance, and the day-to-day work of a high-traffic store. Along the way the site got twice as fast.

Before and alongside that, Spencer spent more than five years building websites for other businesses.
